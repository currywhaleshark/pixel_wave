# Stage 2 — Jellyfish Meadow generation prompts

All selected sources are concept/raw inputs. The builder performs native-resolution reconstruction, palette locking, binary-alpha cleanup, seam-cap composition and packing.

## Sea (`sources/sea-long-source-v1.png`)

Create an extremely wide continuous twilight underwater panorama for a three-screen-wide 1440×270 side-scrolling game background. Water only: dusty periwinkle near the top through blue-violet to deep indigo-plum below, broad soft haze masses, restrained caustic ribbons and low contrast for bullet readability. Keep both edge tones similar for loop repair. No creatures, reef, seabed, plants, bubbles, foreground silhouettes, text, UI, fake pixel blocks or hard horizontal bands.

## Far (`sources/far-long-source-v3.png`)

Redraw the distant jellyfish meadow as one ultra-wide, native shallow bottom strip on pure #00FF00. Preserve a sequence of low valley, small rise, valley, medium rise, long valley, one broad high rise and low breathing space. Use muted periwinkle ground, sparse violet grass and tiny rooted lantern tips in hard-edged countable pixel clusters. Content stays within the bottom 6–14%; the high rise is about twice the valley height. No flat ribbon, repeated stamps, floating creatures, gradients, antialiasing, water, text or vertical squeezing.

## Mid (`sources/mid-long-source-v2.png`)

Redraw one connected midground meadow natively inside a shallow bottom strip on pure #00FF00. Alternate long low clearings and three unequal rises using indigo-violet ground, lavender ribbon grass, cup anemones and occasional rooted lantern buds. Content stays within the bottom 8–18%; medium rises are about 1.5× and one broad rise about 2× the low meadow. Use asymmetric crowns and irregular spacing. No continuous wall, repeated rhythm, detached plants, floating creatures, gradients, antialiasing or squeezing.

## Near (`sources/near-long-source-v2.png`)

Redraw one connected foreground meadow natively inside a shallow bottom strip on pure #00FF00. Use deep plum-indigo ground, flowing violet ribbon grass, lavender cup anemones and a few rooted pink-violet lantern stalks. Keep low meadow within the bottom 8–13%, one integrated landmark near 21% and another near 30%, with long visibly low clearings before and after both. Each landmark must be a single purpose-drawn formation with a broad base and asymmetric crown. No evenly spaced hills, stacked clumps, floating creatures, bright white lamps, gradients, antialiasing or geometric squeezing.
