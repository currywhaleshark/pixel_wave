## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept  
Asset type: source artwork for a three-screen-wide 1440x270 side-scrolling underwater game background  
Primary request: Create an extremely wide continuous open-ocean underwater panorama with calm natural variation across the full width. It will sit behind coral reef parallax layers in Pixel Wave Stage 1.  
Scene/backdrop: clear tropical seawater only, brighter turquoise in the upper zone transitioning to deep muted blue below; broad soft underwater haze masses, very restrained distant caustic bands and faint suspended-light texture.  
Style/medium: clean low-detail game background concept intended for deterministic native-resolution palette quantization; use broad connected color shapes, not large fake pixel blocks.  
Composition/framing: extreme 16:3 panoramic strip; continuous edge-to-edge water field; distribute subtle variation over the whole long width without a central focal point; upper, middle and lower depth zones must all fit inside the strip.  
Color palette: turquoise, cyan-blue, muted ocean blue, deep navy-blue; low saturation and lower contrast than characters and bullets.  
Lighting/mood: calm luminous tropical depth, gentle and unobtrusive.  
Constraints: background water only; no transparency; no horizon line; no hard horizontal band boundaries; no seam at the center; no vignette; left and right edges should have similar average tone so they can be seam-repaired for looping.  
Avoid: coral, reef, rocks, seabed, fish, mermaids, creatures, plants, bubbles, particles, foreground silhouettes, sun disk, text, UI, borders, frames, checkerboard, magenta, painterly brush noise, dense detail, strong highlights, high-contrast streaks, large pixelated squares.
