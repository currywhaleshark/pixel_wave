# Stage 5 Shipwreck Graveyard generation log

All sources are intermediate concept/motif inputs. The build script reconstructs them at the native 480×270 world scale, locks the palette, forces binary alpha on terrain, repairs wrapping edges, and adds seam-cover caps.

## Sea

Ultra-wide empty murky teal underwater field with long horizontal currents and sparse shafts. No terrain, organisms, wrecks, bubbles, text, UI, or frame.

## Far terrain

One low connected seabed ridge with tiny wreck silhouettes: ribs, snapped masts, half-buried hulls. The playfield remains open and every shape is fused to the floor.

## Mid terrain

One connected rolling graveyard shelf with clearly different half-buried wreck forms: bow, stern, exposed ribs, anchor, and mast. No floating fragments.

## Near terrain

One connected foreground shelf built from distinct large fragments rather than repeated whole ships: broken bow cross-section, rib cage, snapped keel, stern/propeller housing, mast stump, and embedded planks. One broad 2–3× height landmark is allowed while the center corridor remains open.
