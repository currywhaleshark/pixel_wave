# Stage 6 Storm Surface generation log

All generated images are intermediate sources. `tools/build_stage6_background.py` reconstructs them at the native 480×270 world scale, locks the palette, forces binary terrain alpha, repairs wrapping edges, and adds native-size seam caps.

## Sea

Ultra-wide empty storm-gray water just below the surface. Bright steel-blue upper water fades to slate navy below. Only broad currents and dim light shafts; no terrain, organisms, bubbles, lightning, text, UI, or frame.

## Far terrain

One low connected blue-slate seabed ridge with small slanted stone fins and sparse sea grass bent left by the current. Long low intervals keep the gameplay corridor open.

## Mid terrain

One connected storm-eroded shelf with diagonal ledges, hooked outcrops, and two broad double-height landforms. Every motif is fused to the floor.

## Near terrain

One opaque connected foreground mass with long low passages, rounded boulder banks, and broad two- and three-times-height wave-cut landmarks. No floating debris or thin towers.
